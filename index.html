Dog Encyclopedia

Meet three friendly breeds. Each card shows a simple illustration and short, everyday care notes.

Beagle

  • Size: Small to medium, about 13–15 inches tall.
  • Personality: Curious, cheerful and friendly with everyone.
  • Care: Needs daily walks and a secure yard—their nose loves to wander.

Golden Retriever

  • Size: Medium to large, about 21–24 inches tall.
  • Personality: Gentle, smart and eager to please.
  • Care: Loves exercise and brushing; great with families and kids.

Poodle

  • Size: Comes in standard, miniature and toy sizes.
  • Personality: Clever, alert and surprisingly athletic.
  • Care: Curly coat needs regular grooming; very trainable.

Illustrations on this page are project-created SVG drawings, not real dog photographs.
